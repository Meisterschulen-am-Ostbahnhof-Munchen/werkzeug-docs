## 🎯 Role of Measuring & Diagnostic Tools in the Curriculum

Measuring and diagnostic tools are **essential tools** in the mechatronics trade. The following diagnostic and testing equipment is required for master craftsman trainees according to the 2025 curriculum framework (Part I LE 2.1, LE 2.2 & Part II LE 1.3):

---

## 1. 🔍 Overview of all diagnostic and measuring tools in the Wiki

### 1.1 Oscilloscopes

- **Application:** Signal waveform analysis on the CAN bus (CAN-High / CAN-Low), hysteresis determination for PWM proportional valves, Hall sensor ignition signals.
- **Link in the Wiki:** [`elektronik/oszilloskop/index.md`](../elektronik/oszilloskop/index.md)

### 1.2 Multimeters & Combination Measuring Devices

- **Application:** Precise measurement of DC/AC voltage, resistance, continuity testing, insulation resistance measurement on high-voltage systems.
- **Link in Wiki:** [`elektrik/messgeraete/index.md`](../elektrik/messgeraete/index.md)

### 1.3 CAN Testers & Protocol Analyzers

- **Application Area:** PEAK adapter cables, bus masters, AEF-ISOBUS-CHECK tool for monitoring PGNs, frames, and bus idle status.
- **Link in Wiki:** *(See also Wiki 3 ISOBUS Technology)*

### 1.4 Digital Inspection Microscopes

- **Application Area:** Optical quality control of SMD solder joints, cold joints, crimps, and cross-sectional images.

### - **Link in Wiki:** [`elektronik/loeten/sonstiges/Mikroskop.md`](../elektronik/loeten/sonstiges/Mikroskop.md)

## 2. ⚡ Workshop Infrastructure & Laboratory Power Supplies

### 2.1 Current-Limited Laboratory Power Supplies

- **Function:** Supply of defined voltages (e.g., 12V / 24V) with adjustable current limiting to prevent component damage during the initial testing of control units.
- **Link in Wiki:** [`elektronik/netzteil/index.md`](../elektronik/netzteil/index.md)

### 2.2 Compressors & Solder Paste Dispensers

- **Function:** Compressed air supply for pneumatic desoldering units, paste dispensers, and workshop cleaning.

# FNIRSI HS-02

### Attachment for the FNIRSI HS-02 Soldering Station and Tips C115, C210, C245

The **FNIRSI HS-02** is an intelligent, portable soldering station characterized by its high performance and ease of use. With a power output of 100 watts and a temperature range of 100°C to 450°C, it is suitable for a wide variety of soldering applications.¹² This soldering station offers both precision and general soldering capabilities and is compatible with C245 tips, but not with C115 and C210 tips.¹²

... #### **FNIRSI HS-02 Features**

- **Power**: 100 Watts
- **Temperature Range**: 100°C - 450°C
- **Heat-Up Time**: ~4 seconds
- **Display**: 0.96-inch IPS display
- **Modes**: Automatic standby and sleep modes¹²

#### **Tip Compatibility**
- **C115 Tips**: These tips are suitable for high-precision soldering of small components. They are ideal for applications performed under a microscope and offer the highest precision³. However, the FNIRSI HS-02 is not compatible with these tips¹².
- **C210 Tips**: These tips offer a good balance between precision and power. They are suitable for general precision soldering but are not supported by the FNIRSI HS-02¹².
- **C245 Tips**: These tips are designed for general soldering and offer a wide range of tip shapes and sizes. They are ideal for a variety of soldering applications, from fine to larger solder joints.¹² The FNIRSI HS-02 supports these tips, providing high flexibility and versatility for soldering.¹²

#### **Tip Similarities and Differences**
- **Similarities**: All three types utilize JBC's advanced technology, enabling rapid temperature recovery and extended tip life.³ They benefit from the FNIRSI HS-02's sleep and sleep modes, further extending tip life.¹²
- **Differences**: The C115 tips are designed for the smallest and most precise work, the C210 tips offer a good balance for precision work, and the C245 tips are suitable for general soldering.¹²³ The FNIRSI HS-02, however, only supports C245 tips.¹²

The FNIRSI HS-02 soldering station is therefore an excellent choice for various soldering applications that require both precision and versatility. With its compatibility with C245 tips, it offers a comprehensive solution for professional soldering work.

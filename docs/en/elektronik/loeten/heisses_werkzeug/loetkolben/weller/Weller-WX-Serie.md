# Weller WX Series

In the author's opinion, the best soldering station in the world is the

Weller WX,

with Weller RT soldering tips.

These tips can be changed while hot,

the heat-up time is a sensational 5 seconds,

and even a small soldering tip can be used for relatively large jobs; the station delivers extremely high heat.

No other manufacturer makes such powerful tips: for example, the RTU032SMS can deliver up to 150W of power!

Unfortunately, this equipment comes at a price:

[https://www.weidinger.eu/de/p/wl48188](https://www.weidinger.eu/de/p/wl48188) The station with handle and tip costs around €1000,

each soldering tip costs between €30 and €40.

# miniware TS-100
The miniware TS-100 is a portable, USB-powered soldering iron with a 65W output. It features digital temperature control, allowing for precise setting and maintenance of the soldering temperature. The TS-100 is small and light enough to be easily transported and used anywhere soldering work is required. It is popular with electronics enthusiasts and professionals and has received good reviews and recommendations in forums and communities.
As mentioned above, Weller is the Mercedes of soldering irons, but it's in a price range that isn't something you buy on a whim.
Further down, we'll see that many soldering stations from Asia are simply junk or offer very poor performance, in addition to very high wear and tear.
A very good compromise is offered by miniware with its TS-100.
This is available as:

* Soldering iron set with one tip,
* Soldering iron set with one tip and power supply,
* Case with soldering iron set with 9 tips

Useful accessories include various carrying cases if you plan to use the device while traveling.

There is also an adapter cable for operating the soldering iron with an 18V battery, which can be quite useful because you don't always need a power supply for small repairs.

Of course, there are also adapters for powering it from a 12V cigarette lighter socket.

## Advantages:

From the perspective of [@franz-ms-muc](https://github.com/franz-ms-muc)], the advantages are as follows:

* High performance
* ATTENTION! The soldering iron can be used with various power supplies. The performance decreases with the power supply's wattage.
* Compact
* The soldering station is essentially integrated into the handle of the soldering iron.
* Easy to take with you when traveling
* Integrated tip
* As with Weller, the temperature sensor and heating element are integrated into the tip.
* This results in faster heating times and more precise temperature control

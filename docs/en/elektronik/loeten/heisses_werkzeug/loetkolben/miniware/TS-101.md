# miniware TS-101

The miniware TS-101 is the latest successor to the successful TS-100 soldering iron. This new soldering iron offers a multitude of improvements compared to its predecessor. One of the most significant innovations is its enhanced performance, achieved through the use of state-of-the-art thermal paste. This allows the TS-101 to heat up faster and more precisely, making soldering considerably easier.
Another key feature of the TS-101 is its improved handling. Thanks to a new, ergonomic handle design and a lightweight construction, the soldering iron is lighter and more comfortable to hold. The soldering iron's controls have also been improved, enabling precise temperature adjustment.
The miniware TS-101 also features an integrated LED display that shows the current temperature of the soldering iron. This allows the user to monitor the temperature at all times and ensure it is always at the correct level.
Overall, the miniware TS-101 is a state-of-the-art and powerful tool that significantly simplifies and improves soldering. It's the perfect choice for anyone who wants to achieve high-quality results and work more efficiently and precisely.
The TS-101 is a copy of a copy,

or a reverse copy.

After Pinecil copied the TS-100 and replaced the USB interface with USB-C,

miniware implemented the same change in the TS-101.

Advantages of the TS-101 over the TS-100:

- Larger display
- Same soldering tips as the TS-100
- Can be powered via AC adapter (barrel jack like the TS-100) or USB-C
- ATTENTION
- Barrel jack: 65W like the TS-100
- USB-C: only 45W
- Special USB-C: 90W
- Quick tip change like the TS-80 via ejector ring

## 90W for the TS-101

ATTENTION! It's not that simple.

You need a PD 3.1 power supply with 28V and 90W. These are the 140W devices.

Additionally, the cable must be rated for 240W.

Many power supplies only go up to 20V.

Software version 2.0 or 2.01 must also be installed.

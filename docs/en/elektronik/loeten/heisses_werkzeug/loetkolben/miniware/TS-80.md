# miniware TS-80
The TS-80 was released after the TS-100, but it is not its successor.
It differs from the TS-100 in its soldering tips. Here, miniware copied the Weller RT soldering tip system and incorporated a 3.5 mm jack plug.
The advantages over the TS-100 are obvious:

* No Allen key is required to change the soldering tip.
* The front ring allows the tip to be ejected even when hot.

The disadvantage is the slightly lower power output of the TS-80 compared to the TS-100.

A mobile phone charger is used as the power supply; in the newer version, one with Power Delivery (PD).

Note that standard mobile phone charging cables are so stiff that they are not pleasant to use. A silicone USB-C cable is required.

## TS-80 Versions

### **TS-80**

The TS-80 is the older model, which supports Quick Charge. [https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quick_Charge](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quick_Charge)]

### **TS-80P**

The TS-80P, on the other hand, also supports PD (Power Delivery). [https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Serial_Bus#Höhere_Leistungen](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Serial_Bus#H%C3%B6here_Leistungen), [https://www.elektronik-kompendium.de/sites/com/1809251.htm](https://www.elektronik-kompendium.de/sites/com/1809251.htm)]

# Infrared Rework Station T862
The **IR Rework Station T862++** is a versatile and powerful station designed for reworking SMD and BGA components. Here are some key features and specifications:

- **Power**: The station has an output power of 800 W².
- **Temperature Range**: The infrared lamp temperature is adjustable from 100 °C to 350 °C, while the preheating plate can reach temperatures from 60 °C to 200 °C².
- **Power Supply**: It operates with an input voltage of AC220V/50Hz or AC110V/60Hz².
Various attachments and accessories are available for the T862++, specifically designed for this station. These include:

- **Focusing Unit**: Enables precise work by accurately focusing the infrared radiation².
- **Various Lenses**: Lenses with different diameters (e.g., 28 mm, 38 mm, 48 mm) are available, which can be used depending on the size of the component being processed.²
- **PCB Holder**: A holder for printed circuit boards that need to be stabilized during the rework process.²

These accessories are usually available directly from the manufacturer or from specialized distributors.

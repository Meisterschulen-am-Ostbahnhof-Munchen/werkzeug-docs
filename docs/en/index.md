# Wiki 5: Tool & Measuring Equipment Handbook (Manufacturing, Measuring & Diagnostic Tools)

## Master Craftsman Schools at Munich East Station

---

> 🛠️ **Definition of Tools in our Documentation:**

By "tools" we mean the **entire range of workshop equipment** – not only production tools (soldering, crimping), but especially **all diagnostic and measuring tools** (oscilloscopes, multimeters, CAN testers, digital microscopes) as well as the **workshop infrastructure** (laboratory power supplies, ESD protection, compressors).


---

### 📋 [This is stated in the curriculum (Framework Curriculum 2025)](./das_steht_im_lehrplan.md)

> **Soldering, circuit board assembly, crimping techniques, and measuring instruments are the main practical skills of the Framework Curriculum 2025** for the production and repair of cable harnesses, connectors, control units, and mechatronic assemblies (LandBauMechMstrV of September 9, 2024, effective August 1, 2025).
